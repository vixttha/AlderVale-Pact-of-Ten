# Storyworld foundation

## Known direction

Ten schools: Fire, Water, Lightning, Frost, Wind, Earth, Light, Dark, Metal, Plant. Ten cultural strongholds are a starting geography, not a limit; villages, ports, wilderness, ruins, neutral towns, mixed regions, islands and later continents may exist. Geography and server realms are independent. Existing Breeze/Horizon/Everpeak realms and strata are implementation references, not newly approved lore. The six creature origins and thirteen player races are in the registry; species and civilization are distinct. No elemental race assignment is assumed.

## City candidates

Veyrath — renewal after disaster; Orsenn — navigation and hospitality; Tavrik — controlled invention; Iskeld — shelter and memory; Avelis — freedom and travel; Durnovar — collective duty; Serevan — education and restoration; Nhalem — privacy and remembrance; Korr — engineering; Ossara — stewardship. Names and fictional founding accounts await owner approval. See public design linked in README. Every city needs cultural institutions, economy, history, distinct architecture, social tensions and relationships.

## Campaign and history — candidate

See [the proposed Pact and campaign foundation](pact-of-ten.md). Founder identities, calendar, origins of races and creature classes, exact chronology and competing historical accounts remain open. Do not convert working explanations into locked events. Record era IDs, event IDs, locations, participants, causes, effects, knowledge boundaries and canon status. Distinguish objective history from in-world belief and player-specific outcomes.

## Living world

Candidate layers: regional trade routes, guilds, food, festivals, language, relationships, law, jobs, housing, everyday interactions, exploration secrets, optional quests and consequences. Hidden discoveries should be meaningful and coherent; keep spoiler contents in private planning unless authorized for public disclosure. Candidate cigarette-smoking interaction is a fictional optional adult activity; content-rating and audience review before implementation. No final reward or animation contract yet.
