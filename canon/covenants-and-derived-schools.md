No new law, name, character, fusion, city, symbol or gameplay approved by this request. The accepted Pact meaning, EA-001 and FQ-001 remain unchanged. Public non-spoiler design; private plot details excluded.

## AV-COVENANT model

A founding covenant is a voluntary relationship between an independent magician and an independent mythical being, not ownership, blood descent, a magical access grant, a divine vote or an inviolable spell. Magicians negotiate and answer to communities; Sovereigns embody a domain, can dissent, leave, or refuse aid, and are not universally invincible. The designation recognizes a foremost known mythical representative, not the only being in the domain. The treaty can operate without any one individual; no divine veto, automatic succession or inherited magic rights. Distinct relationships should have different histories and tensions; not every pair is friends or a combat partnership. Modern offices may outlast original individuals; lifespan, origins, fate, replacement and ceremonial customs remain open.

## One pair at a time — narrative quality worksheet

For each pair preserve stable founder, mythic, city and coalition IDs. Require: (1) original name and in-world linguistic rationale; distinguish invented meaning from real etymology; (2) formative experience and personal desire, fear, flaw and moral limit for each individual; (3) creature anatomy, habitat, motion, material, sensory behavior and independent motives, with AV-3D-001 feasibility before asset production; (4) first meeting, disagreement, reason for voluntary cooperation, cost or sacrifice and treaty contribution; (5) ties to other founders, races, settlements, economy and present-day quest implications; (6) symbolism derived from events rather than arbitrary decoration; (7) distinguish documented event, disputed legend, present truth and private reveal; (8) continuity review against Pact, timeline, EA-001/FQ-001 and existing NPC/quest IDs, then explicit owner acceptance. ## Derived schools: decision candidate

A school of study is an educational/cultural institution, distinct from a fundamental elemental affinity or a combat recipe. Preserve exactly ten permanent base elements, five opposition pairs, maximum two equipped affinities, all ten selectable at creation and separately earned fusion techniques. A derived tradition can emerge from practitioners of two schools, organize a guild/academy, develop a local material culture or settle a region. Descent means intellectual/cultural lineage by default, not literal divine reproduction or mandatory bloodlines. Independent societies can share a recipe or have no recipe; a new recipe need not create a city, tradition or being. Additional base-school damage/resistance types, recursive/triple fusions, or a third equipped affinity would change existing architecture and require explicit owner decision and migration assessment.

Model candidate: stable tradition ID, display name, parent school IDs (two distinct base schools for a fusion-derived tradition), optional recipe references (by stable ID), independent cultural status, optional settlement IDs, optional mythic exemplar IDs, provenance, version and approval status. Registry relationships are many-to-many; base schools remain authoritative in Element HQ; Universe HQ owns narrative history and geography. No automatic runtime import or access grant. Derived traditions may be discovered as lore before playable recipes exist; teaching/casting any actual fusion requires FQ-001 gates, separate owner kit approval, implementation evidence and earned access. Future mythic exemplars may be related to two Sovereigns by shared domain or influence, but biological origin, ability and species require separate review. No guaranteed strongest global ranking. No playable entry created.

Illustrative Rust tradition: Water movement and moisture plus Metal surfaces motivate a culture of inspecting and preserving bridges and tools. Rust's real oxidation context is distinct from invented controlled magic; actual combat effect/cost/counterplay still unverified. A settlement might sit on a river trade route with repair guilds, rather than a monochromatic Rust city. No Rust academy, territory, creature or kit approved. Sugar and Magnetism remain selected recipes only.
