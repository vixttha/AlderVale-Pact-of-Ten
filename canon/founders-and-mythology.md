# Founding peoples and living mythology — v0.0.3 candidate

Owner accepted the meaning of the Pact on 2026-11-24 and requested mortal magician leaders alongside mythical representatives. All new names, biographies, dates, beings and institutions are proposed, not approved gameplay or art. Non-spoiler summary.

## Conflict audit

Ten denotes schools, not kingdoms, races or gods. Ten founding delegates negotiated the treaty; beings are independent witnesses, not extra votes or rulers. Membership may grow. Successors appointed by local communities, not necessarily heirs. No magical oath disables casting; shared institutions, mutual aid and accountability sustain the treaty. No being owns a school or restricts access. Apex means foremost known embodiment of a domain, not omnipotent or strongest in every encounter. Lore potency separate from stats, rarity and ownership. One proposed designated apex per base school, other divine beings possible. Guardian is an encounter role, not a seventh class.

Optional hybrid exemplars may be added by stable fusion recipe ID without expanding the ten schools or founding seats; no mandatory exemplar for each recipe. Rust, Sugar and Magnetism remain selected recipes, zero playable, no automatic species approval. FQ-001 and separate owner approval and implementation evidence govern any playable fusion. EA-001 remains all ten selectable at creation, discovery and earned mastery, max two equipped affinities, separately earned fusions. No Time/Space, recursion, race-exclusive magic or compulsory divine bonds. True divine entities are not ordinary inventory or trade goods; voluntary avatar/blessing concept remains open. Opposition is not political allegiance. Existing NPCs are not retroactively founders; legacy migration #187 stays independent.

## Creature connections

AV-CLASS-001 wildlife: habitat and migration predate treaty. AV-CLASS-002 magical beasts: adaptive organisms, rescue and voluntary trust. AV-CLASS-003 elementals: animate matter, source care and rituals. AV-CLASS-004 spirits: memories, places, unresolved obligations. ## Campaign implications

Each stronghold has a civic problem, regional history, ecology and optional school discovery. Earth builders and Wind couriers can cooperate without forcing dual school access. Primary-only player viable with allies or ordinary tools. Mythical beings appear via rare encounters, artifacts, stories and voluntary dialogue; not ten required boss kills. The three ideological positions remain candidate; private antagonist identity, reveal sequence and endings are withheld. More regions and civilizations can join. No new species, stats, model or runtime approval. AV-3D-001 applies when production authorized.
