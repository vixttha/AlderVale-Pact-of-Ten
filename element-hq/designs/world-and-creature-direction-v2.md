## AV-3D-001 — 3D production law (owner-directed)

All visible world and creature model assets should ultimately use intentional game-ready 3D models, with animation where the asset has motion or gameplay states. Use AI-assisted 3D generation and our Blender Agent (BART) as appropriate, with refinement and validation before game integration. No placeholder or raw model-generation output counts as production acceptance. Preserve existing approved art, silhouettes and colors; never silently substitute a generic asset. This law establishes the production direction, not a mandate to animate static rocks, require a paid provider, or discard legacy assets today. No new spending, releases, merges or gameplay expansion authorized.

Proposed acceptance workflow: design brief -> owner-approved concept and turnaround -> provenance/rights record -> AI or handcrafted base mesh -> BART/Blender inspection, cleanup, retopology, UV/material and scale review -> rig and skin where needed -> animations -> optimized GLB/glTF export -> headless checks and browser load/animation/attachment regression -> visual owner acceptance. Static assets need scale, origin, collision and appropriate materials; animated props have state clips, creatures have rig-appropriate idle, movement, attack, hit reaction, defeat, interaction and optional special motions. Do not force all species onto one skeleton; use anatomy-specific rigs and attachment sockets. Include source and output hashes, generator/model/license, tool/version, mesh budgets, textures, material channels, pivots, bounding boxes, clip names and review receipts. Budget per asset class after measurements; do not infer GPU performance from software CI. Standard public hosted runners for supported Blender/GLB validation only; no GPU/paid generation without explicit authorization. BART's verified published README describes v0.0.3 inspection, creation, conversion and repair with headless Blender; automatic creature-quality rigging/animation remains unverified. Current game Rowan GLB has 66 joints and 16 authored clips; procedural fallback retained; existing scripts validate source hashes and clips. Character Forge's distinct race anatomy and universal humanoid wardrobe rules remain separate. Existing approved brand: Woodland Fantasy title/menus and Pact Heraldry seals/milestones/rewards; preserve workmark source. No verified universal final creature/world color palette or master rig; recover exact approved references before color or asset lock. See main art/branding/WORKMARK_V1.md, docs/HUMAN_MODEL.md, scripts/asset-report.mjs and character-forge-hq anatomy contract. Historical 15-school palettes cannot silently become the ten-school palette.

## Six creature classes — owner-supported direction, mechanics proposed

Each class has a separate origin, behavior and acquisition method; monster is hostile encounter, guardian/boss is a role, not a species. Divine title does not imply rarity or unconditional power. Existing EA-001 school access does not require owning a creature. No automatic fusion species.

| Class | Ecology and purpose | Acquisition route | Transfer policy proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wildlife | Animals, pollinators, scavengers, prey; ordinary or elemental adaptation | Observe, feed appropriate resource, rescue or trust quest; some ambient-only | Eligible domesticated/adoptable individuals only |
| Magical beast | Elemental biology; evolved/symbiotic anatomy, territorial or social behavior | Track signs, encounter, rescue or nonlethal trial, then pact | Eligible pact transfer after consent conditions |
| Elemental | Animate matter sustained by a magical source | Stabilize source, demonstrate control, perform ritual | Transfer only portable anchored beings; site-bound ones stay |
| Spirit | Memory, place, emotion or nature-bound supernatural presence | Resolve unfinished oath or quest; freely offered pact | Personal or place-bound pacts not sold; eligible pact transfer only |
| Construct | Crafted vessels, awakened relics and automata | Obtain design and parts, build/repair, awaken; independent sentience respected | Craftable vessels or eligible awakened constructs |
| Divine being | Mythic entities, primordial guardians or deities with motives | Story challenge, pilgrimage, covenant or blessing | Deity not ordinary inventory; blessing/avatar separately scoped, generally bound |

Every species definition needs stable ID, origin/class, habitat, behavior, primary affinity or explicit none for ordinary fauna, encounter role, rarity, acquisition/transfer rules, combat eligibility, animation family, equipment socket map, and art provenance. Species != unique instance != encounter definition. Instance has persistent ID, owner/realm/wizard, nickname, level/XP, bond, maturity/form, trait allocation, cosmetics, transfer state and revision; stats derived from trusted versioned definition, not accepted from client. Ambient fauna have rarity and descriptive ecological stats; power displayed as noncombat or 0 rather than fake combat strength. Non-bondable bosses have separate encounter power and stats, no owned instance. Divine avatars not automatically approved.

## Progression, rarity and power — candidate

Proposed rarity ladder: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic. Rarity measures availability/discovery difficulty; not raw DPS multiplier. Variant (color/pattern), species role, level, maturity, bond and condition independent. Every combat-eligible creature has level, health, attack, defense, accuracy, resistance and role-specific potency (e.g. healing/support); speed/initiative only if meaningful in existing turn rules. Do not invent a positional or real-time mechanic just to use speed. Start with current HP/attack and add only stats with defined formulas, limits and counterplay. Existing main has baseHp/baseAttack, three fixed stage names, branch A/B, talents, chromatic variants and bond thresholds; new schema must migrate explicitly without loss. Distinguish growth/maturity (species-specific), combat XP/level, bond trust and traits. Cosmetic items not stats. Power rating derived, transparent, versioned and only a matchmaking/inspection estimate, never stored as editable authority, never rarity or price. Evaluate damage, protection and support over fixed benchmark encounters; display role-specific bars and compare equal levels, separate threat rating for bosses. Exact formula, caps and point economy require playtests. Illustrative level-12 equal-budget creatures: rare striker 220 HP, 70 attack, 30 defense; common defender 280 HP, 45 attack, 55 defense. These are invented examples, not balance targets.

## Customization — candidate

Wardrobe slots: headwear (hats/crowns), neckwear (collars/scarves), body (harness/cape), charm (tags/ornaments), optional limb/tail/wing slot where anatomy supports it. Items have compatible rig families, fit variants, socket/local transform, clearance masks, dye channels, ownership and display priority; non-compatible items have alternative fit or explicit unavailable state, never force a human hat onto a formless elemental. Allow nickname, compatible color accents and curated patterns without overriding species identity or rarity variants. Preserve hitbox, attack visibility, silhouette and base stats. Test attach/detach, growth changes, skinning, idle/motion/attack clipping, remote rendering and save/reload. Keep hats and other cosmetics tradeable independently when eligible. Do not conflate creature wardrobe with owner-approved universal playable-race clothing rule.

## Creature exchange — candidate

Reuse Bazaar principles but not gear quantity model. Current main server/market.ts postListing validates GEAR_BY_ID and wallet counts, and buyListing atomically settles gear; this is not a creature marketplace. New in-game Florin-only unique-instance listing, licensed handlers/NPC adoptions, direct gifting/trades later if authorized. No real money or random paid packs. Allow some creatures to buy/sell; no forced monetization, sale of non-consenting sapient beings, deity ownership, quest-bound companion or mandatory starter sale. Market displays species, level, rarity, visible stats, cosmetics included/excluded, transfer policy and price. Preserve original provenance and personal nickname or chosen rename; bond to new owner re-established, level/maturity and learned traits preserved under explicit policy, never reset silently. Repeated transfer cannot duplicate rewards or XP. Acquisition of a creature never buys school mastery or a fusion.

Server transaction: verify authenticated seller, realm, wizard, instance, version, eligibility, not in duel/active slot/escrow, unequip or require safe replacement (never strand player); create listing + freeze instance, reject second listing. Buyer same realm, sufficient trusted Florins, valid capacity, not self-buy; atomically debit/credit, transfer unique ownership, settle listing once, enforce revision and idempotence. Cancellation unfreezes exactly once; races with purchase resolve one outcome. No client stat/price injection, cross-realm transfer, negative/overflow price, public private IDs, escrow equip, double sale or reward duplication. Guest sandbox NPC acquisition allowed if approved; no shared player market or offline sync into live economy without separate migration. Start with one eligible NPC acquisition and one test instance transfer; actual economy, fee, price cap, bond transfer and capacity remain open. Use existing server-authoritative tests and public runners when implementation approved.
