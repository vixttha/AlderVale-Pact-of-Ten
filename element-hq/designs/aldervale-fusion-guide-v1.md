## How a pact makes an egg

In the proposed version, two companions shape a shell together at a place where their affinities meet. Fire softens stone at the caldera and Forest threads living roots through it. When the shell closes, both companions return to the party and the player has an egg. The hatchling becomes a permanent third creature with one derived affinity and its own growth stages. The bestiary can remember which companions helped form it.

| Step | What the player sees and does |
| --- | --- |
| Discover | Follow a creature behavior or landform from the bestiary. Reaching the site reveals the pairing and its role in the encounter. |
| Prepare | Select two eligible companions from the current wizard and realm. Read their two jobs and preview the guaranteed species and trait choices. |
| Complete | Use both creatures during a short encounter. Success yields one egg for the attempt. The pair stays available on failure and retry. |
| Hatch | Complete a short, thematic objective tied to that egg. Choose its starting play style with a preview. |
| Return | Revisit the encounter for the other style or follow a different clue. Keep a stable bestiary record of each pairing and outcome. |

### The first four encounters

| Fusion | What the companions do | What makes each hatchling worth raising |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Magma** | Open a vent with Fire. Anchor the path with Forest. During incubation, move the shell onto warm ground before the vent cools. | Try **Lava Surge** for a focused creature strike or **Stone Crust** for a ward on the wizard. Preview both with existing moves. A later appearance pass can distinguish glowing fissures and bark-like armor. |
| **Tempest** | Route a shallow current with Water. Use Lightning to charge the stones along the safest path. | Develop a damage-first branch that rewards target positioning and a support branch that prepares the wizard's next attack. Existing Arc Tide and Nimbus Coil can test the role split. |
| **Glacier** | Raise water to a marked ledge. Freeze it for a crossing with Frost. The pair must keep the route stable as swimmers pass. | Give this line the defensive job. Start with Glacial Crush or Rime Sanctuary. Test wider ward coverage as a distinct later ability. |
| **Eclipse** | Dark screens an exposed crossing. Light reveals the safe markers one by one. | Build around creating an opening or taking advantage of one. Start with existing Corona Fang and Lunar Siphon. Exposing hidden enemies is a separate combat mechanic. |

For Magma, start with one location, two companion actions, and one egg. Test whether players understand what each companion contributed, why they chose a hatch style, and which pairing they want to pursue next. The first attempt should fit one play session with a visible objective. Keep rank locks on wizard cards and let the newt show its own creature move immediately.

## Before advancing to gameplay

1. Decide whether the field pact is the Magma prototype.
2. Decide which state the hatch choice sets and whether the Arbor can change it.
3. Verify a pair-specific preview, both companion actions, a stable reward claim, recovery after reload, and the first creature move on one prototype checkout.
4. Review the trial with a playthrough before choosing the next fusion or any additional affinity.

The current milestone is the existing guest first-playable loop. This design prepares a later gameplay decision.
