# Hearth Gate R2 — carved sanctuary candidate

Owner requests a further refinement of R1, specifically a distinctive flame and ancient civic emblem feeling. R1 remains preserved and the preferred concept remains Hearth Gate; no final lock. Source baseline 7d9c4e7d9fc5a53d33d22f48a9cd50f66f04691b. Manual vector construction (image generation unavailable).

## Changes

- Broad gate shoulders, flared masonry feet and carved inner bevel give architectural weight. Remove diamond decorations and stacked decorative bands.
- Two structural shoulder joints and paired pillar joints evoke real masonry, not arbitrary runes. A single keystone represents collective construction. No new canon event or numeric symbolism.
- Asymmetric two-tongued flame with a sweeping inner ember evokes containment and recovery: one flame, not two elements, not a creature portrait. This is a candidate artistic association with Maren's controlled heat and Avarn's thermal cycles, not a new fact about the Sovereign.
- Dark hero has identical geometry and a higher-luminance palette. Simple icon strips ornament; monochrome has a true transparent cutout. Keep one identity in four roles.
