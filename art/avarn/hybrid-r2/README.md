## Phase 3 — candidate physical mechanics
Fictional bounded thermal cycle: resting/banked (shutters closed, dim glow), intake/recovery (pads sense gradients; plates slowly adjust), regulated release (localized shutters open, controlled glow), cooldown (shutters progressively close; recovery interval and abrupt-cooling vulnerability). No unlimited heat or mechanical fuel grate. Slow heavy alternating tripod gait A=L1,R2,L3 / B=R1,L2,R3; start with conservative overlapping support and adjust foot timing from measured support polygon and center of mass. Middle pair supports long trunk rather than ornamental extra legs. Turn by shortening inner steps and lengthening outer steps; plant before weight transfer. Foot pads compress, splay subtly and retain ground contact; no skating. Neck pivots through flexible folds; jaw opens independently; crown follows skull; overlapping scutes rotate/slide locally, never scale rigid plates to fake breathing. Tail counterbalances low, flexes in sequence, never intersects legs. Breathing moves soft underlayer and modestly shifts plate edges. Warning rumble and thermal pulses communicate intent; no inferred human speech. Animation candidates: idle, walk, turn, settle, warning, controlled vent, recoil, interaction; not a combat kit. Joint limits and timings remain prototype hypotheses, not validated mechanics.
